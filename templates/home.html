Nelson Mandela Radio

The Nelson Mandela Radio, an initiative of the Nelson Mandela African Institute of Science and Technology (NM-AIST), stands as a beacon of knowledge dissemination, innovation promotion, and societal progress. Established in response to the imperative need to bridge the gap between academia, industry, and society, this radio station embodies the essence of excellence in broadcasting, education, and outreach.
