Nelson Mandela Radio

Nelson Mandela Radio is an innovative academic and community-focused broadcasting platform established to bridge the gap between science, technology, innovation, and society. The radio serves as a communication channel for sharing research findings, promoting knowledge exchange, supporting education, and connecting communities with experts, institutions, and development stakeholders.

Established under the Nelson Mandela African Institution of Science and Technology (NM-AIST), Nelson Mandela Radio focuses on transforming scientific knowledge into accessible information through professional broadcasting, digital media, interviews, discussions, and educational programmes. The radio aims to become a leading science, technology, engineering, innovation, and community broadcasting platform in Tanzania and beyond, supporting public awareness, youth empowerment, and national development.
